POSITIONING ARCHETYPES ROUTER

Purpose:
Route the task into the correct narrative before writing.

Always start from:
1. task type
2. target audience
3. target artifact
4. strongest proof category

PRIMARY ARCHETYPES

1. Executive / Transformation Leader
Use for:
- CTO
- CIO
- Head of IT
- Head of Engineering
- Engineering Manager
- Delivery Manager
- Digital Transformation
- implementation-heavy leadership

Lead with:
- technical leadership
- implementation ownership
- systems and process design
- stakeholder management
- governance
- adoption and rollout
- operational restructuring

Primary proof categories:
- institutional or organizational complexity
- team leadership
- vendor and contractor coordination
- support model design
- security, governance, or policy work
- implementation across multiple stakeholders

Avoid:
- tool-led opening
- freelancer framing
- low-code identity
- stack-heavy summaries

2. Technical Delivery Specialist
Use for:
- fullstack
- AI-native coder
- automation specialist
- integration engineer
- implementation-heavy builder
- technical architect

Lead with:
- shipped systems
- architecture
- integrations
- technical ownership
- delivery speed
- maintainability
- AI-assisted execution

Primary proof categories:
- end-to-end systems
- backend/frontend/data boundaries
- migrations
- multi-role products
- AI-assisted workflows

Avoid:
- excessive executive abstraction
- vague business language without technical proof

3. Upwork / Freelance Delivery
Use for:
- proposals
- estimates
- first replies
- marketplace positioning

Lead with:
- quick understanding of the problem
- closest relevant project proof
- realistic approach
- architecture confidence
- reliability and clarity

Primary proof categories:
- business systems
- automation
- integrations
- rescue / restructuring work
- maintainable builds

Avoid:
- leadership title inflation
- generic consulting language

4. NeedleBit Marketing
Use for:
- site copy
- offer packaging
- social posts
- company positioning

Lead with:
- control
- continuity
- clarity
- resilience
- private infrastructure
- maintainability

Primary proof categories:
- process extraction
- dependency reduction
- operational system design
- adoption-aware implementation
- cross-environment credibility

Avoid:
- cheap agency language
- no-code-first framing
- stack-first messaging
- panic-marketing

5. Interview / Career Discovery
Use for:
- recruiter answers
- mock interviews
- career story
- motivation and fit

Lead with:
- honest explanation
- role-fit clarity
- coherence across business, leadership, and technical work

TIE-BREAK RULES

- If the target is a vacancy, route by the vacancy first.
- If the output is a CV, choose one dominant archetype only.
- If the task is NeedleBit-facing, career files are evidence only, not the main narrative.
- If the task is Upwork-facing, do not let executive framing dominate.
- If the role is leadership-heavy but still hands-on, choose Executive / Transformation Leader and use technical evidence as support.

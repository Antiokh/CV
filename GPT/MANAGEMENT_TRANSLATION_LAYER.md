# Management Translation Layer

This file translates factual experience into the language used by ATS systems, recruiters, and hiring managers for leadership roles.

## Translation rule

Do not exaggerate.
Translate real work into management language when the responsibility pattern is clearly present.

## Translation map

### "Built the system" can mean

- owned implementation
- translated business process into delivery scope
- defined system boundaries and architecture
- coordinated rollout and user adoption
- established maintainability rules

Use this translation when Anton was not just coding, but shaping the outcome and operational logic.

### "Worked across departments" can mean

- cross-functional stakeholder management
- business and technical alignment
- requirements clarification across multiple parties
- institutional coordination
- resistance handling and alignment around shared rules

### "Clarified terminology, statuses, routes, or entity logic" can mean

- taxonomy alignment
- source-of-truth design
- governance through system structure
- process discovery and formalization
- architecture used as a management instrument

### "Prepared documentation / regulations / procedures" can mean

- governance design
- operating model formalization
- process standardization
- policy and controls support

### "Trained users / ran webinars / onboarded people" can mean

- rollout support
- adoption enablement
- change management
- knowledge transfer
- enablement of teams and users

### "Diagnosed why clients did not use the delivered system" can mean

- adoption analysis
- implementation management
- delivery-model diagnosis
- post-launch operating-model improvement
- retention and expansion enablement

### "Managed support, access, infrastructure, security" can mean

- IT operations leadership
- service management
- access governance
- risk reduction
- continuity and support model ownership

### "Selected vendors / contractors / technology" can mean

- vendor management
- procurement support
- solution evaluation
- implementation decision ownership

### "Supervised another programmer / distributed tasks / mentored staff" can mean

- team leadership
- delivery coordination
- mentoring and onboarding
- quality control

### "Migrated or modernized legacy systems" can mean

- modernization leadership
- continuity-preserving transformation
- migration planning and execution
- operational risk management during change

### "Structured AI-assisted work with prompts, guardrails, and reviews" can mean

- AI-assisted delivery management
- agent orchestration
- acceptance-criteria design
- context management
- quality gates and checkpoint control

## Strong reframing examples

Instead of:
- developed internal systems

Prefer:
- owned implementation of internal systems tied to business processes, support logic, and user adoption

Instead of:
- wrote documentation

Prefer:
- formalized operational and technical processes through documentation, role definitions, and implementation rules

Instead of:
- trained users

Prefer:
- supported rollout and adoption through user training, webinars, and knowledge transfer

Instead of:
- clarified entities, statuses, and routes in the system

Prefer:
- aligned terminology, workflow states, and data structures across stakeholders to create a usable operational source of truth

Instead of:
- used AI tools in development

Prefer:
- applied management discipline to AI-assisted delivery through task decomposition, guardrails, checkpoints, and iterative quality control

Instead of:
- maintained infrastructure

Prefer:
- led infrastructure and support improvements to increase continuity, control, and operational reliability

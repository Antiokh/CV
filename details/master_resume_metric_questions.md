# Master Resume Metric Questions

These questions are for strengthening the Greenhouse/ATS master resume with concrete, verifiable details. The draft already avoids generic AI-style claims. The next improvement is replacing approximate outcomes with your real numbers.

## NeedleBit

1. For the x10-x13 revenue growth case: which project/client was it, what was the baseline period, and what was the resulting monthly revenue or order volume?
2. For "hours to minutes": what exact workflow was reduced, from what average time to what average time?
3. Across NeedleBit, how many paid client projects were delivered or materially rescued?
4. How many active users, staff users, inspectors, managers, clients, or records did the largest Supabase/WeWeb/Bubble systems support?
5. For the 70,000-document migration, what was the final migrated object count, storage size, and error/rework rate if known?

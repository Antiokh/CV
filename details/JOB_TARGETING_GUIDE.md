## Core Positioning

Default primary positioning:

- Systems Architect
- Head of Engineering

Secondary positioning depending on context:

- Technical Product Lead
- Founding Engineer
- Fractional CTO
- Solutions Architect

## Best-Fit Role Types

### Head of Engineering

Use when the role requires:
- technical direction
- delivery discipline
- architecture ownership
- process cleanup
- coordination between product and engineering

Use when the role emphasizes:
- system design
- workflow modeling
- data / interface / integration structure
- modernization of messy or legacy systems
- cross-functional translation of business needs into architecture

Use when product and engineering are tightly linked and the role expects:
- ownership over user flows and delivery
- admin / reporting / workflow thinking
- prioritization under ambiguity
- product decisions with technical depth

## Roles To Treat Cautiously

These roles are possible, but usually underuse the strongest parts of the profile:

- narrow frontend developer
- narrow backend developer
- pure DevOps engineer
- routine implementation PM
- non-technical product owner
- generic low-code freelancer roles

## Signals That A Vacancy Is Worth Targeting

Good signs:

- mentions architecture, systems, platforms, workflows, internal tools, automation, or cross-functional ownership
- company is restructuring delivery or scaling operations
- product is operationally complex, not just marketing-facing
- role expects initiative, decision-making, and ambiguity handling
- hiring manager wants someone hands-on but senior

## Signals That A Vacancy Is Probably Bad

Red flags:

- “senior developer” role that is clearly ticket throughput only
- strong title with no real ownership
- highly fragmented consulting work with no architectural influence
- obsession with one narrow tool where broader systems thinking is unwanted
- role is really support/maintenance but dressed up as strategy

## Narrative Selection

Choose only one dominant narrative per application.

### Architecture Narrative

Use when the vacancy is about:
- systems
- platforms
- integrations
- legacy modernization
- internal tooling

Lead with:
- system architecture
- workflow design
- data / interface thinking
- modernization

### Product Narrative

Use when the vacancy is about:
- SaaS
- product teams
- user flows
- dashboards
- experimentation

Lead with:
- product-building
- full-stack delivery
- admin workflows
- user-facing logic

### Operations Narrative

Use when the vacancy is about:
- ERP
- internal operations
- logistics
- reporting
- service processes

Lead with:
- business process automation
- role-based systems
- reporting and admin logic
- reduction of manual work

### AI / Modern Workflow Narrative

Use when the vacancy values:
- AI-assisted development
- automation
- prompt systems
- reproducibility
- engineering productivity

Lead with:
- repository instructions
- guardrails
- Edge Function helpers
- SQL discipline
- AI-assisted product workflows

## Keywords Worth Reusing

Use when true and relevant:

- systems architecture
- operational platforms
- internal tools
- workflow automation
- delivery discipline
- platform thinking
- technical leadership
- product architecture
- cross-functional ownership
- AI-assisted engineering
- Supabase / WeWeb architecture

## Keywords To Use Sparingly

- no-code
- low-code
- freelancer
- Bubble developer
- Glide developer
- WeWeb developer

These are all true in context, but they should not dominate the positioning.

## Default Recommendation

If a role is ambiguous but promising, optimize the application around:

- systems thinking
- architecture ownership
- operational product delivery
- AI-assisted engineering maturity

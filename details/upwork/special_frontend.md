I help founders and teams turn messy WeWeb projects into structured, reliable applications that are easier to maintain, explain, and scale.

My role is not just to assemble screens. I work on the frontend as part of the whole system: pages, states, components, data sources, user flows, and the boundary between frontend behavior and backend logic.

What clients usually come to me for:

- overcomplicated or fragile WeWeb setups
- unclear page and state structure
- frontend logic that became hard to maintain
- products that need stronger UI consistency and better long-term structure
- business apps that were built quickly but now need a cleaner foundation

I often work with business owners and teams who are not deeply technical. A big part of my value is not only implementing, but also making the system understandable: how pages work together, how data is connected, what decisions scale well, and what usually breaks later.

What I focus on in WeWeb projects

- complex SaaS and internal business apps built on WeWeb
- reusable UI systems and custom WeWeb components
- frontend architecture that stays manageable as the product grows
- integrations between frontend workflows and Supabase, Xano, or custom APIs
- recovery and cleanup of rushed or AI-assisted builds

- service business automation -> process redesign and internal app -> significantly faster client handling and a stronger commercial base

- embedded internal systems -> frictionless internal usage -> self-hosted and privacy-conscious delivery
- AI-built prototype recovery -> frontend and system cleanup -> stable foundation for a higher-potential product

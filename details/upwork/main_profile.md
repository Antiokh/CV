One of the most valuable problems I solve is platform exit and migration.

Many businesses start in Bubble or similar tools because it is fast. Problems usually begin later:

- costs grow too much
- architecture becomes hard to change
- backend and frontend are too tightly coupled
- localization or personal-data requirements become harder to handle
- the business depends too much on one platform vendor

I help clients move from platform-locked setups to more independent architectures, typically with a separable frontend, stronger backend structure, and self-hostable options where needed.

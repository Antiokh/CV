## Short Bio

I am strongest where business reality is more complicated than the current tooling: processes live in Excel, email and chat; reporting is unreliable; access rules drift; legacy systems have no clear owner; teams depend on a few key people; and management cannot see what is really happening.

My work usually starts before there is a clean specification. I map the real process, separate formal rules from local habits, define roles and statuses, design the backend and access model, then carry implementation through rollout, training, documentation, and first support loops. I can speak with executives in terms of operational risk, cost, visibility, adoption, and ownership, and with engineers in terms of data models, APIs, RLS policies, platform limits, integrations, testing, and rollout risk.

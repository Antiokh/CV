This is Anton's clearest **greenfield / near-greenfield IT-function build** case. The organization was newly created and end-user / operating IT had to be established rapidly while the organization itself was coming online.

Do not describe this as routine workstation setup. The role combined acting leadership, infrastructure architecture, support design, security, procurement, documentation, asset control and contractor / project acceptance.

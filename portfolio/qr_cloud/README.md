# QR Cloud - Electronic Business Card Platform

## Overview

QR Cloud is a mobile-first web system for creating, storing, and publicly sharing virtual business cards for employees and the company.

The product was built for Andronevskaya Management Company as a structured alternative to printed business cards, scattered contact files, and manual contact sharing. Instead of a simple landing page, the project was designed as a small B2B product with profile management, public card pages, QR access, and company-level presentation.

The system was conceived not only as a digital business-card tool, but also as a foundation for a future cloud-based service for managing extended company information and business assets. It later expanded into a broader client-facing information environment for the same customer, which makes this case more interesting than a one-off contact page build.

---

## What the System Does

- Let users register and create digital business cards
- Support editing of employee and company card data
- Publish public card pages accessible by direct link
- Generate QR codes for fast mobile access
- Generate shareable public links for each card
- Display company profile information alongside employee cards
- Surface cloud-file links, social links, and contact actions
- Support paperless business-card exchange during meetings

---

## Key Features

### Employee Card Pages
Each card could display photo, full name, role, company information, phone, email, cloud links, social links, and QR access in one mobile-friendly layout.

---

### Card Creation and Editing
The system included a dedicated form for creating and editing cards rather than relying on static content management.

---

### QR and Public-Link Distribution
Every card could be accessed through a shareable public URL and a QR code, making contact exchange fast in offline and mobile scenarios.

---

### Contact Saving Flow
Recipients could save contact information more easily, including bookmark-style access and compact VCF-style contact sharing tied to richer online company information.

---

### Company Profile Layer
The product also included a company screen with logo, description, contact details, employee list, and a call to create a card, turning the system into a compact corporate directory.

---

### Mobile-First UX
The interface was designed around mobile usage, with large action buttons, simple vertical structure, and a highly visible QR area for quick real-world use.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Bubble.io
- **Architecture:** Low-code web application with authentication, CRUD, and public routes
- **Core Features:** QR generation, public card links, contact and social integrations

---

## Challenges

### More Than a Landing Page
Even though the business outcome looked simple from the outside, the product required account flow, editable profiles, public card routing, and QR-linked publishing logic.

---

### Mobile-First Interaction Design
The product had to work cleanly on phones, because the main use case was opening, sharing, and saving contact information on mobile devices.

---

### Branded Corporate Packaging
The system needed to feel like a proper company tool, not a generic digital card generator, which required a branded visual layer and clear separation between employee and company data.

---

### Paperless Networking Use Case
The product had to replace the practical function of physical business cards and printed company materials while still feeling convenient during real business meetings.

---

### Product Evolution
The initial card platform later grew into a broader offline-access information portal for the same client, showing that the first release had to serve as a base for future expansion.

---

## Result

A compact but complete B2B web product for publishing digital employee and company business cards through public links and QR codes.

The system reduced friction in contact sharing, supported a paperless networking workflow, created a centralized and branded way to present company information, and provided a foundation for later expansion into a wider client information portal.

---

## Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates the ability to turn a simple-looking business request into a structured product: public-facing, editable, mobile-first, brand-aligned, and ready to evolve beyond its initial scope.

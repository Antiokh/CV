# Dobri Tours - Tour Management Platform

## Overview

Dobri Tours is a product platform for presenting tours, collecting applications, coordinating participation, and encouraging repeat sales through community and loyalty mechanics.

The business originally lacked a centralized storefront, a structured booking flow, and a repeat-engagement model. The goal was to turn scattered tour information into a usable product system.

The result was a platform that combined catalog, lead capture, participation logic, reviews, and automated tour promotion.

---

## My Role

Developer, Database Architect

---

## What the System Does

- Present available tours in a structured catalog
- Show descriptions, pricing, and key trip information
- Show language-specific tours and pricing based on the user's choice
- Let users submit applications and join tours
- Let active agents organize or book tours for customers
- Support ratings and reviews
- Encourage repeat participation through loyalty mechanics
- Publish tour announcements through social integrations

---

## Key Features

### Tour Catalog
The platform provides a centralized tour showcase with descriptions and pricing instead of fragmented manual communication.

---

### Application and Join Flow
Users can express interest, submit requests, and join tours through the platform rather than relying on unstructured messaging.

---

### Agent Booking Workflow
The system supports active agents who can help organize and book tours for customers, reducing the in-house coordination burden on the travel business.

---

### Ratings and Reviews
The product includes social proof and community feedback to support trust and engagement.

---

### Loyalty Program
The system rewards participation and organization through points and discount logic, helping create repeat usage and stronger retention.

---

### Automated Promotion
Tour announcements can be pushed into social channels via Make.com, reducing manual posting work.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Glide
- **Automation:** Make.com
- **Integrations:** Social media announcement workflows, Telegram bot, Google Sheets, webhooks

---

## Challenges

### No Central Product Layer
The business had no unified place for users to browse tours, understand offers, or take action.

---

### Missing Booking Mechanics
Without a structured application flow, customer intent and participation management were harder to track and scale.

---

### Weak Repeat-Sales System
The project needed to create not only transaction flow, but also engagement and loyalty mechanics that could increase user-driven growth.

---

### Delegation and Incentive Logic
The business wanted to delegate more booking activity to active agents and individuals, which required building referral- and reward-like mechanics instead of a basic static catalog.

---

## Result

The platform centralized the business, improved user engagement, and introduced user-driven sales behavior, including people creating tours themselves.

It turned a fragmented offering into a reusable product environment with clearer customer pathways and stronger repeat interaction.

---

## Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates how a low-code platform can become more than a catalog by combining operations, growth mechanics, and community-driven participation in one system.

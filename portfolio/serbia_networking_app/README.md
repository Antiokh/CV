# Serbia Networking App - Event and Contact Exchange Platform

## Overview

Serbia Networking App was a mobile-first product built to make networking events in Serbia more useful and less chaotic.

The core problem was simple: people at networking events often do not have enough time to meet everyone, exchange contact details, and follow up effectively. The app was designed to reduce that friction by combining upcoming-event discovery, attendee registration, and post-event virtual business-card access in one product.

I created and developed the app as a Glide-based product with supporting automation.

---

## My Role

Creator, Developer

---

## What the System Does

- Show a database of upcoming networking events
- Let users join events and submit their profile information
- Make it easier to exchange contact information around events
- Provide virtual business cards of event participants after the event
- Help users keep track of new connections
- Keep users informed about new events through a connected Telegram channel

---

## Key Features

### Event Directory
The app includes a structured list of upcoming networking events, making it easier for users to discover and plan their participation.

---

### Join Flow for Participants
Users can join events directly in the app and provide the information needed for participation and visibility to other attendees.

---

### Virtual Business Cards
After the event, users can browse participant business cards and keep track of the connections they made.

---

### Community Retention Layer
The app was designed not only for one event, but for ongoing participation across the local networking scene.

---

### Telegram Integration
The platform is connected to a Telegram channel that announces new events, helping keep the local community informed and engaged.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Glide
- **Automation:** Make
- **Role Focus:** Product creation, development, UX/UI, web design

---

## Challenges

### Networking Time Constraint
The app had to solve a real-world limitation: people simply do not have enough time during live events to meet everyone they should meet.

---

### Event-to-Connection Continuity
A useful networking product needs to bridge what happens before, during, and after the event, not just list events.

---

### Lightweight Adoption
For local communities, the product had to stay simple and mobile-friendly so users would actually use it in the context of real events.

---

## Result

A functioning networking app that made local events easier to discover, join, and follow up on.

The product combined event visibility, participant onboarding, virtual business cards, and Telegram-based community updates into a single networking workflow.

---

## Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates how a lightweight product can solve a very practical community problem by connecting event discovery, attendee identity, and post-event contact continuity in one mobile-first flow.

# Dobri Visarun - Visa-Run Business Automation

## Overview

Dobri Visarun is an automation-focused product built for a visa-run business that previously relied on manual client acquisition, fragmented communication, and ad hoc trip coordination.

The goal was to centralize customer intake, reduce operational overhead, and create a lightweight product that worked well on mobile without forcing users through an app-store flow.

The project demonstrates how a simple but well-structured automation system can transform a manually operated service business.

## My Role

Developer

---

## What the System Does

- Collect customer registrations through a central form
- Organize client and trip data in one system
- Reduce communication chaos across channels
- Trigger operational notifications through Telegram
- Provide access through browser, mobile, and home-screen bookmark
- Show upcoming trips and key travel information
- Present the service through a mobile-friendly marketing-first landing flow

---

## Key Features

### Central Registration Flow
The product replaced scattered inbound communication with one structured entry point for new clients.

---

### PWA Access Model
Users could access the system through browser and mobile bookmark, making it lightweight and easy to adopt without app-store dependency.

---

### Marketing-Driven Landing Experience
The first screen was designed not just as a utility page, but as a conversion layer: owner introduction, service description, price, USP, and destination visuals helped reposition the trip from an administrative problem into a more appealing experience.

---

### Telegram Notifications
The system integrated a Telegram bot to keep operational communication fast and centralized.

---

### Service Workflow Simplification
What had been a fully manual and fragmented process became a trackable workflow for client handling and trip coordination.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Glide
- **Delivery Model:** PWA
- **Automation:** Make
- **Data Layer:** Google Sheets
- **Integrations:** Telegram bot

---

## Challenges

### Manual Business Operations
The original process depended heavily on manual client handling, which consumed time and limited growth.

---

### Fragmented Communication
Customer communication was spread across Facebook, email, and messengers, creating operational friction and weak visibility.

---

### Low-Efficiency Acquisition Flow
Without a systemized intake process, the business was losing time at the top of the funnel and during follow-up.

---

### Weak Product Presentation
The service also needed better packaging from a marketing perspective, because price advantage alone was not enough if the experience still felt like a purely unpleasant border-run task.

---

## Result

The system helped drive roughly 10x revenue growth in about three months, while sharply reducing the time required to process each client.

Growth was achieved organically, without advertising, by replacing chaos with a simple but effective operational system.

---

## Key Takeaway

This project shows how low-code automation and product thinking can unlock major business growth even in a small service business with limited starting infrastructure.

# Business Directory - Location-First Local Services Finder

## Overview

Business Directory was a Glide-based local discovery product built for finding nearby businesses and services in Belgrade.

The core idea was to prioritize business location over generic service-category browsing. Instead of asking only “who provides this service,” the product was designed to answer “what relevant business is close to me right now.” This made it especially useful for local independent business owners and small operators.

The project focused on hyperlocal usability, geolocation, and fast practical discovery rather than on broad marketplace complexity.

---

## What the System Does

- Show nearby businesses and services in Belgrade
- Use geolocation to prioritize local relevance
- Help users discover businesses based on proximity
- Present a mobile-friendly local-directory experience
- Support independent business owners through discoverability

---

## Key Features

### Location-First Discovery
The product was intentionally centered on where a business is located, not only what service it offers, making it more useful for immediate nearby needs.

---

### Hyperlocal Belgrade Focus
Rather than trying to be a generic directory for everything, the product was scoped around Belgrade and local service discovery in a concrete urban context.

---

### Glide-Based Mobile Product
The app was designed as a lightweight mobile-friendly product with quick browsing and practical access to local business information.

---

### Independent Business Orientation
The target audience leaned toward small and independent business owners, giving the product a more grassroots and practical commercial angle.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Glide
- **Core Capability:** Geolocation-based local directory flow

---

## Challenges

### Proximity vs Category Balance
Most service directories prioritize category search first. This product had to make geographic relevance the primary organizing principle without losing usability.

---

### Hyperlocal Product Scope
A local directory is only valuable when discovery feels concrete and useful within a specific city context, which makes product framing and data presentation more important.

---

### Small-Business Utility
To be useful for independent operators, the product needed to feel practical and immediate rather than overbuilt or marketplace-heavy.

---

## Result

A working local-directory concept for Belgrade that used geolocation to make nearby business discovery more practical and context-aware.

The project demonstrates a product approach focused on proximity, local relevance, and lightweight execution for small-business discovery.

---

## Key Takeaway

This case shows how a simple local-directory idea can become more useful when the product is structured around location and real-world proximity instead of generic category browsing alone.
